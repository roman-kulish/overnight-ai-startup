# Mortgage vs. The Machine

A social experiment in vibe-coding the most absurd "overnight AI startup" ideas YouTube ever sold us.

## The Backstory

I am a developer of a certain vintage. I still write actual code, I still stare at stack traces, and I still believe software should compile before you try to sell it.

And yet — like many people with a mortgage and a heartbeat — I have watched the thumbnails.

> "How I Made $50,000 Last Month With One AI App."  
> "Teenager Earns $200k Passive Income. Here's the Blueprint."  
> "Any Housewife Can Build a Seven-Figure Empire Overnight. No Code. No Skills. Just This."

So I did the reasonable thing: I stopped watching and started digging. Then I wrote about what I found:

**[Mortgage vs. The Machine: Inside the YouTube "Overnight AI Startup" Grift](https://medium.com/@roman.kulish/mortgage-vs-the-machine-inside-the-youtube-overnight-ai-startup-grift-8b3833f24146?sharedUserId=roman.kulish)**

The article is not a callout piece. It is a look at the elegant, repeatable, extremely modern economics of the AI money genre. The product is rarely the business. The content about the product is.

Which brings us to this repository.

## The Experiment

I have compiled a database of the most popular, generic, copy-paste AI startup ideas pushed by overnight gurus. For each project I randomly mash 2–3 of those ideas together and vibe-code a series of killer, doomed-to-be-successful web applications.

If a single AI idea can bring in "bags of cash," then a volatile cocktail of two or three must be pure uranium. It's an instant millionaire recipe: just add cash and stir slowly.

## Live Site

🚀 **vibe-coding.shiretechpartners.com.au**

Each application fulfills all the baseline requirements of a classic overnight YouTube hustle:

- It looks expensive.
- It pulls real-time external data.
- It leverages a cheap LLM to do the heavy lifting.
- It has a massive, hopeful **"Buy Me a Coffee"** button.

## The Apps

| App | The pitch |
|-----|-----------|
| **Meditate to Your Shares** | Enter a stock or crypto ticker. Get a guided meditation generated around its recent volatility. |
| **AI Automation Agency Generator** | Enter any mundane hobby. Get a fully-realized, entirely fictional €10k/month agency blueprint. |
| **VC Roast Pitch Deck** | Submit a one-sentence "billion-dollar AI idea." Get roasted like a Series A pitch. |
| **Crypto-Astrology Oracle** | Zodiac sign + crypto ticker. Real-time chart + astrological explanation. |

## Why?

Because the experiment is more fun than fighting the algorithm, and honestly, my mortgage statement still stares at me with quiet judgment.

If the whole thing fails and I don't wake up mortgage-free on a superyacht wrapped in bikini babes, the absolute least my audience can do is keep me caffeinated.
