# AI Automation Agency Generator

## Concept

A parody of the "AI Automation Agency" hustle gurus. Enter a mundane hobby and get a slick, 5-step business plan promising $10k/month.

- **Input**: `Goat walking`
- **Output title**: "B2B Caprine Asset Management"
