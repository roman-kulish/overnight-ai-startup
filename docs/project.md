# Project

## What

A public web dashboard hosting four parody AI applications. Each app mocks a common YouTube "overnight AI startup" archetype by taking a silly input and producing a polished, deadpan AI-generated result.

## Why

The project is the practical half of an essay about the YouTube AI money genre. The real business in that genre is rarely the AI product — it is the content about the AI product. This experiment turns the observation into a deployable joke.

## Origin story

See the source story on Medium:

## The four apps

1. **Meditate to Your Shares** — stock ticker → sarcastic guided meditation
2. **AI Automation Agency Generator** — hobby → fake $10k/mo agency blueprint
3. **VC Roast Pitch Deck** — idea → brutal VC-style roast
4. **Crypto-Astrology Oracle** — zodiac + crypto ticker → astrological price reading
